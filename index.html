DISTRACTION DEFENCE

ANDROID SETUP
IMPORTANT SYSTEM LIMITATION

ARISE can't block YouTube by itself.

A website or home-screen PWA can't control or freeze other Android apps. Android's built-in Digital Wellbeing can do this. Set it up once here, then activate it before study sessions.

METHOD A · FOCUS MODE

BEST FOR STUDY
  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  3. Open Focus mode.
  4. Select YouTube and any distracting social apps (for example Instagram or other short-video apps).
  5. Tap Turn on now, or set a schedule for your study hours.
When Focus mode is on, selected apps are paused and generally can't be used normally until Focus mode is turned off. Menu names may differ slightly by phone.

METHOD B · APP TIMER

DAILY LIMIT
  1. In Digital Wellbeing, open Dashboard or App timers.
  2. Choose YouTube.
  3. Set a small daily limit that you choose in advance.
  4. Repeat for other apps that distract you.

App timers limit the selected app; Focus mode is more useful for blocking distractions during a specific study block.

BEFORE YOU START STUDYING

ARISE can't verify the Android setting. Tick this after you turn it on in Settings.

EXTRA FRICTION (OPTIONAL)

Remove YouTube from your home screen, turn off nonessential notifications, and keep your phone across the room during study. If you need YouTube for lectures, consider using planned lecture playlists and activating Focus mode for everything else.
